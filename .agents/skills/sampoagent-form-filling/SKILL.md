---
name: sampoagent-form-filling
description: Safely assist with application forms through a configured browser agent.
---
Classify questions before filling. Low-risk known fields may be prepared; medium-risk fields require review according to mode. For the local SampoAgent worker only, a required unresolved low/medium scalar field can be collected through the application-scoped Questions section in Applications; its answer is explicitly confirmed and bound to the exact application, form, and verified listing, never to the reusable answer bank. Codex-operated browser forms must still pause and ask the user when a required fact is unknown. CAPTCHA, login, legal, medical, security, immigration, conflicts, unsupported controls, and access denials require manual action. Never bypass controls.
